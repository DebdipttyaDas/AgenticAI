# Staff / Senior Distributed Systems & Cloud Engineer

## Overview
We are looking for a Staff / Senior Distributed Systems Engineer to design, architect, and scale our core real-time transactional platform processing over 100,000 requests per second.

## Core Requirements (Must Haves)
- 5+ years of production experience designing and maintaining high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems using Go, Rust, or Python.
- Deep hands-on experience with container orchestration (Kubernetes, Docker) and cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP).
- Proven track record of architecting fault-tolerant microservices, handling data consistency, and designing resilient database schemas (PostgreSQL, Redis).
- Experience leading architectural reviews and mentoring senior engineering team members.

## Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Haves)
- Hands-on experience building event-driven streaming pipelines using Apache Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Prior experience with gRPC, Protocol Buffers, and distributed tracing systems (OpenTelemetry).
- Contributions to open-source systems software or high-scale infrastructure tooling.
